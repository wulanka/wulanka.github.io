U.S. Nuclear Plant Lifecycle Decision Analysis
01 / Overview

About the project.

Explored alternative pathways for nuclear power plants: extending existing operations, replacing capacity with small modular reactors, or decommissioning.

Approach & contributions

Applied multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA/AHP) and sensitivity analysis across cost, emissions, reliability, safety, climate resilience and technology readiness.

Findings & resources

This project was completed collaboratively. Selected decision matrices, criteria and findings can be shared here without reproducing the full report.

About access and collaboration: This page summarizes selected aspects of the work. Where applicable, projects were completed with colleagues and institutional partners. For further information, get in touch. Full reports may not be available for distribution.
