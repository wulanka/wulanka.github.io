Zero-Emission Bus Pathways for Campus Mobility
01 / Overview

About the project.

Compared diesel, battery-electric and hydrogen bus options to explore the implications of zero-emission mobility for a campus transit system.

Approach & contributions

Modeled 12-year lifecycle costs, energy consumption and well-to-wheel greenhouse gas emissions. Under the modeled assumptions, battery-electric buses produced approximately 63% lower emissions than diesel buses.
