Product development / Geospatial SaaS / Industrial decarbonization

DecarbAtlas

Connecting industrial emissions, decarbonization technologies, enabling infrastructure, and policy incentives in one geospatial decision-support platform.

Conceptual project illustration; product development and customer discovery are ongoing.

What is DecarbAtlas?

DecarbAtlas is an emerging software-as-a-service (SaaS) concept designed to help users explore and compare industrial decarbonization opportunities. It aims to bring fragmented technical, spatial, and policy information into a more accessible decision-making workflow.

The problem

Industrial decarbonization decisions often require information from separate sources: facility emissions, technology options, electricity and other infrastructure, project economics, and incentives. Bringing these together can take substantial time and make early-stage opportunity screening difficult.

Proposed platform and methodology

The proposed platform combines facility-level emissions data with geospatial layers representing technologies, infrastructure, financing opportunities, and relevant policy incentives. Its intended workflow is to help users identify facilities, understand constraints, and explore potential decarbonization pathways. Features and underlying data coverage are still being developed.

Potential users and customer discovery

The team is conducting customer discovery with potential users including government agencies, industrial companies, economic development organizations, utilities, and consultants. These conversations are intended to test what information is most useful, how decisions are currently made, and where existing tools leave gaps.

My role and current status

I initiated the concept and lead a cross-functional team developing the idea and its user-centered research direction. DecarbAtlas is participating in the Zell Lurie Institute’s Product Innovation Program. The project is in development; a finished platform and validated outcomes are not yet being claimed.
