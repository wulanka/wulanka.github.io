Industrial Decarbonization in Wayne County
01 / Overview

About the project.

Working with Wayne County’s Department of Sustainability & Innovation through the Dow Sustainability Fellows Program to examine industrial emissions and practical pathways for decarbonization.

Approach & contributions

Building a facility-level emissions database for 25 major industrial emitters using SQL, Python and GIS; evaluating technology readiness, costs and policy incentives; developing an economic model for potential cap-and-invest scenarios.

Findings & resources

Research in progress. Findings and partner-approved materials will be added as available.
