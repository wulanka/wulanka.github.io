2025 / Renewable Energy / Municipal Infrastructure

Coldwater
Solar Carport
Feasibility Study.

Evaluated the feasibility of solar carports at municipal sites in Coldwater, Michigan, examining system design, energy production, project economics, and opportunities for implementation and expansion.

Coldwater, Michigan Solar PV & infrastructure SketchUp · NREL SAM · PVsyst Feasibility & implementation planning
01 / Project overview

Turning parking spaces into
energy infrastructure.

Solar carports offer municipalities an opportunity to generate renewable electricity while maintaining the existing function of parking areas. This project explored the technical and economic feasibility of implementing solar carports at municipal facilities in Coldwater, Michigan.

The research combined solar carport design, energy production modeling, site assessments, and implementation planning to evaluate a potential pilot and opportunities for future expansion.

164 MWh Approximate modeled annual electricity generation

02 / Design development

From concept
to configuration.

Developed and compared two solar carport configurations using SketchUp, considering parking arrangements, structural layout, solar panel placement, and site-specific constraints.

These design alternatives provided the foundation for evaluating electricity production and assessing how different configurations could fit within the municipality's existing infrastructure.

03 / Technical analysis

Modeling the
energy potential.

Modeled solar photovoltaic performance using NREL's System Advisor Model (SAM) and PVsyst to estimate electricity generation and evaluate the proposed system's performance.

The 119.6 kW pilot was estimated to generate approximately 164,000 kWh annually, representing around 38% of the municipal building's electricity consumption. These estimates helped inform the project's feasibility and implementation considerations.

04 / Scalability

Looking beyond
one parking lot.

Beyond the pilot, the project examined opportunities for expanding solar carport infrastructure across five municipal sites. The assessment considered the suitability of different locations and how solar infrastructure could support longer-term municipal energy planning.

05 / Research output

From feasibility
to implementation.

A key outcome of the project was the development of a solar carport planning and implementation guide intended to support municipalities interested in evaluating similar renewable energy infrastructure.

The guide brings together practical considerations for solar carport development, including site selection, system design, technical analysis, financial considerations, and steps toward implementation.

Published by the University of Michigan Graham Sustainability Institute. The full feasibility report is not publicly available.

06 / Takeaways

Connecting technical analysis
with local decisions.

This project brought together technical energy modeling, infrastructure design, and municipal planning. Beyond estimating solar generation, the work explored how local governments can evaluate renewable energy investments within existing infrastructure, budgetary, and implementation constraints.

It also demonstrated the value of translating detailed technical assessments into practical resources that municipalities can use when considering future projects.
