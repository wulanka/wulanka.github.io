Coldwater Solar Carport Feasibility Study
01 / Overview

About the project.

Evaluated the feasibility of solar carports at municipal sites in Coldwater, Michigan, considering system design, energy production, costs and implementation.

Approach & contributions

Designed and evaluated two configurations in SketchUp; modeled system performance in NREL SAM and PVsyst. The 119.6 kW pilot was estimated to generate approximately 164,000 kWh annually, offsetting about 38% of municipal building electricity use. Examined expansion across five sites and developed a transferable municipal implementation guide.
