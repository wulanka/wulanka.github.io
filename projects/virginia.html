Virginia Energy Planning & Renewable Siting Analysis
01 / Overview

About the project.

Examined renewable energy siting opportunities in Virginia through spatial analysis of energy demand, generation potential and environmental constraints.

Approach & contributions

Used GIS-based suitability analysis to explore how spatial and environmental factors affect energy planning decisions.
