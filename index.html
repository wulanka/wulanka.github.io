01 / About

A little
about me.

I'm a dual-degree graduate student at the University of Michigan, pursuing a Master of Public Policy (MPP) in International Economic Development and a Master of Science (MS) in Environment and Sustainability, focusing on energy systems.

My work spans local and international projects across the United States, Latin America, Southeast Asia, and South Korea, bringing together policy research, technical analysis, and cross-sector collaboration. I'm interested in how energy, technology, and economic development intersect, and how different policy and institutional contexts shape approaches to sustainable development.
